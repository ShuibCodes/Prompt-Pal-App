Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptPal ("PromptPal", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by [Operator Legal Name], collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use the PromptPal mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

PromptPal is an educational app that helps you learn AI prompt engineering through interactive challenges. To do that, the App relies on a few trusted service providers (described below) to handle sign-in, store your progress, and run the AI that evaluates your prompts.

1.Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the App and save your learning progress.

a. Account information

When you create an account, our authentication provider (Clerk) collects and stores:

  • Your email address
  • Your name (if you provide it)
  • A profile image / avatar (if you set one or sign in with a provider that supplies one)
  • Authentication identifiers and sign-in metadata

You may sign in with email and password, with Google, or with Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, we receive only a private relay address.

b. Content you create

  • The prompts you write during challenges
  • The AI-generated output produced from your prompts (for example, generated images or text)
  • Submission details such as scores, matched criteria, and feedback

c. Usage and progress data

  • XP, levels, daily streaks, quest completions, and attempt history
  • In-app activity needed to power leaderboards, achievements, and daily challenges

d. Device and technical data

  • Device type, operating system, and App version
  • Diagnostic, performance, and error data used to keep the App stable
  • Network information, including an IP address observed by our backend when the App connects

e. Purchase information

If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through in-app purchase and managed via RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status (for example, active or expired) but never receive your full payment card details.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of sensitive personal data, and we ask that you do not include sensitive personal information in your prompts.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Create and secure your account and authenticate you
  • Save your progress, XP, streaks, achievements, and history across sessions
  • Send your prompts to our AI provider to generate and evaluate challenge results
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • Monitor performance, debug issues, and improve the App
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security, or technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3.AI Processing

PromptPal uses Google's Gemini API to generate and evaluate content based on your prompts. When you submit a challenge, your prompt (and, for image challenges, the resulting image) is transmitted to Google for processing. This processing is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy commitments for the Gemini API.

Please do not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in your prompts. AI-generated output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected, and is provided for learning purposes only.

5.Data Retention

We retain your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 7), we delete your associated data from our backend, including your profile, progress, attempts, generated images, and related records. Some information may persist briefly in backups or be retained where required by law.

7.Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and associated data directly in the App, at any time:

Profile → Account → Delete Account

Confirming deletion permanently removes your account and your associated data (profile, XP, streaks, progress, attempts, generated images, and achievements). This action cannot be undone. You may also email [contact email] for assistance.
